Guides

Viewing your AI usage

AI Usage is the window that shows how much of the included AI a monthly membership has used, and what this Mac spent it on. The figures in the diagram were invented for this guide. On the Mac, open the app menu and choose AI Usage…. On iPhone and iPad that reading sits under Settings, Session AI. You can also press and hold the meter row on the live panel.

The gauge is every device

The bar at the top is the share of included AI used in the current period. A paid month labels that period this month. A trial labels it this week. There is no 1-day, 7-day, or 30-day control. The membership is in one period, and the window shows that period.

The percentage is the service's figure for the whole membership, every Mac and every iPhone on it. A polish on the phone and a title on the Mac move the same bar. The window prints a percentage. The terms guarantee no particular allowance, so no token ceiling appears.

What you did with it

Under the gauge, a box names the work the allowance paid for on this Mac. The line counts calls, and in the diagram it reads 126 polishes, 58 titles, and 7 regenerates. Tokens are the unit on the chart below.

Calls on an OpenAI key you supply are a second line. The plan is not charged for them, and they stay outside the gauge. That line reads 12.

The box ends with Counted on this Mac. Calls from the phone sit inside the percentage. They do not appear in this list.

Each day, on this Mac

The columns use the same scope as those counts: this Mac. Each column is one day in the period, stacked by the kind of work. By feature colours them Polish, Titles, and Regenerate. By model recolours those days by which model answered. That pair is the only picker.

The grey column, marked earlier, is usage this Mac has already counted that the chart cannot split into a day, a feature, or a model. It still belongs in the Mac's total.

Before any usage

Until the membership has used the service, the gauge stays off the page, and so does the chart. What remains is the standing note that the service comes with every plan.

Development builds can show a row for the cost of the service. That row is absent from the window everyone else sees, and this guide leaves it undescribed.

The names on the chart

Feature names are the app's own list: Polish, Regenerate, Translate, Feedback, Titles, Sends, and Other. The figure draws three of them. A real window lists only the kinds of work that have actually run on this Mac.

On the Mac and on the iPhone

On the Mac, the command is AI Usage… in the app menu. Holding the live panel's meter row for half a second opens the same window. On iPhone and iPad, the page is under Settings, then Session AI.

Opened from either device, the percentage stays the membership total, and the counts and bars stay the Mac's itemised record. The callouts keep that split visible.

These figures are an example

The gauge at 47 percent, the counts 126, 58, 7, and 12, the days from 1 November through 5 November, and the grey column at 38,000 were drawn for this page. They are a sample. They record no real membership, and they state no size for the allowance.
