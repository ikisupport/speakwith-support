Which engine does what

SpeakWith picks the right tool for the job. Here's what each engine is great at.

Trial & Pro

Apple

Built into macOS. Instant and offline. Many spoken input languages for dictation, ideal for quick notes and everyday use.

Trial & Pro

Whisper

Maximum robustness on tough audio: accents, background noise, crosstalk.

Trial & Pro

Parakeet

Live dictation and fast batch import. Words arrive as you speak; a whole imported file comes back in seconds, with excellent accuracy on clean English audio.

Trial & Pro

Nemotron

Another live option alongside Apple and Parakeet. It streams as you speak, so words keep arriving at the same pace however long you talk.
