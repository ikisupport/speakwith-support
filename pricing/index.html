Which engine does what

SpeakWith picks the right tool for the job. Here's what each engine is great at.

Free & Pro

Apple

Built into macOS. Instant, offline, and free. Perfect for quick dictation and everyday notes.

Pro

Whisper

Maximum robustness on tough audio: accents, background noise, crosstalk. Steps up from a fast model to a high-accuracy model when it senses lower confidence.

Automatic escalation requires the optional accuracy add-on.
Pro

Parakeet

The speed champion. Near-instant transcription with excellent accuracy on clean audio.

Pro

Nemotron

Live dictation in around 40 languages. Choose it when you dictate in German or another language besides English; on English, Parakeet stays a little sharper.

Where to get SpeakWith

The Mac app is sold direct. The iPhone app is free.

App Store

Free on the App Store for iPhone

SpeakWith for iPhone is a free companion recorder, arriving on the App Store soon. Live dictation transcribes on your iPhone with Apple Speech, and your transcripts stay on hand, organised by day. Optional session titles and polish use your own OpenAI key. No subscription.

Direct (Gumroad)

Subscribe, or buy once

The direct build unlocks with a Gumroad membership, billed monthly, that you can cancel any time. If you would rather own it outright, a one-time license is still available. Either way you get the complete, unsandboxed version of SpeakWith, including the switch that types your dictation into other applications. Not sure yet? Start with a 14-day free trial. The Mac download is signed with our Developer ID and checked by Apple's notary service before we upload it.
